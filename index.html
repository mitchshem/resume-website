About Me

I grew up on Long Island surrounded by salespeople. My mom is an optometrist — by age 12, I was shadowing the reps in her optical shop, helping customers try on frames and learning how to upsell designer lenses. My dad managed a car wash, then moved into real estate, and I watched him hustle around the clock to close deals. I picked up serving shifts at Dirty Taco & Tequila and hosting at Marinara Pizzeria — two years in the restaurant industry that taught me how to read people, think on my feet, and keep ten things moving at once.

Then I went and got an electrical engineering degree — because I'm wired to understand how things work at a technical level. After graduating from GWU, I spent two years at Welsbach Electric in NYC running a portfolio of public infrastructure projects. It was project management on paper, but the day-to-day was building client trust, reporting to executives, and turning messy, high-stakes problems into plans people could act on.

Today I'm a Sales Development Representative at Justworks, opening conversations with founders and business owners about payroll, benefits, and HR. It's the job where everything clicks: the curiosity to learn a business quickly, the comfort of talking with decision-makers, and the discipline to show up for a high-volume day, every day — and keep finding ways to do it better.

Outside of work, I'm deep into the AI space — researching new models, tinkering with open-source projects, and building my own tools. More and more of that finds its way back into how I work.
